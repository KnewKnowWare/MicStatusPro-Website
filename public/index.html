MicStatusPro

The visual hardware status indicator for your microphone.

Say goodbye to the "Am I muted?" panic! MicStatusPro is the ultimate visual safety net for remote workers, streamers, and online educators. By communicating directly with your Windows hardware, MicStatusPro places a sleek, click-through colored border around your screen to give you instant, unmistakable visual feedback on your microphone's true status. Never talk for five minutes on mute again, and never worry about a hot-mic accident when you think you're safe.

Why MicStatusPro?

  • The Standard Version: Gives you the core visual border. If the border is red, your Windows hardware is physically cut off. You are 100% safe.
  • Upgrade to Pro: Unlocks the ultimate power-user toolkit, including the floating Text Overlay, Global Push-To-Talk, multi-monitor support, and exclusive access to our beta Insider features.

Features:

  • Visual Confidence: A transparent, click-through screen border instantly tells you if your mic is live or muted.
  • Pro: Push-To-Talk (PTT): Turn any keyboard key into a hardware-level walkie-talkie button.
  • Pro: Global Hotkeys: Toggle your mute status from anywhere, even if your meeting app is minimized.
  • Pro: Multi-Monitor Matrix: The border perfectly duplicates across all your active screens and seamlessly adapts when you dock or undock your laptop.
  • Pro: Custom Color Engine: Match the overlay to your brand or personal aesthetic with full hex-code color selection.
  • Pro: Floating Text Widget: Prefer text? Swap the border for a draggable, transparent overlay that explicitly reads "Mic Muted" or "Mic Active."
  • Privacy First: MicStatusPro operates entirely locally on your machine. We do not collect, listen to, or transmit your audio data.
