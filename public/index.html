MicStatusPro

The visual hardware status indicator for your microphone.

Say goodbye to the "Am I muted?" panic! MicStatusPro is the ultimate visual safety net for remote workers, streamers, and online educators. By communicating directly with your Windows hardware, it places a sleek, click-through colored border around your screen to give you instant, unmistakable visual feedback on your microphone's true status.

Visual Confidence

A transparent, click-through screen border instantly tells you if your mic is live or muted. If the border is red, your Windows hardware is physically cut off. You are 100% safe.

Global Hotkeys

Toggle your mute status from anywhere, even if your meeting app is minimized. Instantly mute or unmute without scrambling to find the right window.

Push-To-Talk (PTT)

Turn any keyboard key into a hardware-level walkie-talkie button. Hold your designated action key down to speak, and instantly cut your mic when you release it.

Multi-Monitor Matrix

The border perfectly duplicates across all your active screens and seamlessly adapts when you dock or undock your laptop.

Custom Color Engine

Match the overlay to your brand or personal aesthetic with full hex-code color selection using the native Windows Color Picker.

Colorblind Support

Colors might not work for you, so we built robust colorblind support. We offer distinct ASCII symbols in the border and an alternate System Tray icon so everyone can benefit.

Floating Text Widget

Prefer text? Swap the border for a draggable, transparent overlay that explicitly reads "Mic Muted" or "Mic Active."

Privacy First

MicStatusPro operates entirely locally on your machine. We do not collect, listen to, or transmit your audio data.
