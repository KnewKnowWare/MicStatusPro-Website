Privacy Policy for MicStatusPro

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

KnewKnowWare, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the MicStatusPro application (the "App").

2. Data Collection and Usage

The short version: The MicStatusPro application does not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal data.

MicStatusPro is designed as a standalone, local desktop utility.

Specifically:

  • No Application Telemetry or Analytics: The MicStatusPro application does not track how you use the app, nor does it collect crash reports or usage statistics.
  • No Personal Information: We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any identifying information to use the App.
  • No Internet Transmission: The App does not connect to the internet to transmit data to our servers or any third-party services.

3. Microphone Access

MicStatusPro requires access to your system’s audio state to function.

  • The App monitors the microphone's mute status (active or muted) to display visual indicators such as the screen border.
  • We do not record, listen to, save, or transmit any audio data. The App only checks the on/off state of the audio endpoint; it does not process the audio feed itself.

4. Local Storage

Your user preferences (such as border color, thickness, and hotkey configurations) are saved locally on your own computer in a standard configuration file (config.json). Any diagnostic error logs generated by the App are also saved purely to your local hard drive. We do not have access to these files.

5. Documentation Website Analytics

The MicStatusPro application itself does not collect analytics or usage data.

However, the MicStatusPro documentation website may use Microsoft Clarity to help us understand how visitors use the documentation and identify areas for improvement. Microsoft Clarity may use cookies or similar technologies to analyze website usage and visitor interactions.

This may include information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Documentation search terms entered
  • Clicks and navigation patterns
  • General website usage behavior

This information is used solely to improve documentation quality, navigation, troubleshooting resources, and search results. No audio, microphone content, or personal conversations are collected.

6. Third-Party Services

Because the App operates entirely locally on your machine, it does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services, advertisers, or tracking networks.

The documentation website may utilize Microsoft Clarity analytics services. This service is used solely to improve documentation usability and content quality.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and, where feasible, providing a notice within the App.
